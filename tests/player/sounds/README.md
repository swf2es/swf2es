Sounds made for the player's tests with FFmpeg's sine source and LAME: a
quarter second of a 440 Hz sine on the left and a 660 Hz one on the right,
at 44.1 kHz stereo and 96 kbit/s without LAME's header frame (`tone.mp3`)
and with it (`tone-tagged.mp3`), and the two mixed to mono at 22.05 kHz
and 32 kbit/s (`tone22.mp3`). They are this repository's own.
