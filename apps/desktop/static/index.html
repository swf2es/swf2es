ActionScript 3 needs two libraries

A SWF's ActionScript 3 links against builtin.abc, avmplus' standard library, and playerglobal.abc, Adobe's flash.* declarations. playerglobal is Adobe's and may not be redistributed, so swf2es does not include it: point swf2es at a copy of your own, once, and it remembers where it is. SWFs without ActionScript 3 play without either.
