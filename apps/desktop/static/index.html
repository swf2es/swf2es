ActionScript 3 needs builtin.abc

A SWF's ActionScript 3 links against builtin.abc, avmplus' standard library, and a playerglobal, the flash.* classes, which swf2es brings its own of. Point swf2es at builtin.abc once, and it remembers where it is; choose a playerglobal.abc only to use it in place of swf2es's own. SWFs without ActionScript 3 play without either.
